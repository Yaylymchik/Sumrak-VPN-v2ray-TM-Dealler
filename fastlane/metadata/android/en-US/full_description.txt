Sumrak VPN (SumraX) is an Android VPN/proxy client built on v2rayNG / Xray. Import a subscription or add servers manually — the app does not sell VPN access or operate its own network.

Why Sumrak VPN:
• Smart Connect — ping servers and connect to the fastest
• Manual mode — lock to your selected server
• Remaining subscription days on the home screen; renewal reminder when expired
• All subscriptions auto-refresh in the background every 30 minutes
• Default Proxy (GET) ping, compact speed on the home screen
• Subscription groups, routing rules, per-app proxy
• In-app update checks (GitHub Releases)
• Push notifications when Firebase is configured
