Sumrak VPN is an Android VPN/proxy client based on v2rayNG. Import your subscription link or add servers manually — the app does not sell VPN access or run its own network.

Features:
• Supports common V2Ray/Xray protocols and subscription links
• Per-app proxy and routing rules
• Subscription refresh, ping, and grouping
• Optional background app update checks
• Push notifications when Firebase is configured by the distributor

You are responsible for complying with local laws and your server provider's terms.
